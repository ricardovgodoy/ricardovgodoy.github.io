### Towards Capability-Aware Traversability Navigation for Unstructured Environments

- **Date:** Monday, September 28, at 2:30 p.m.
- **Session:** Trustworthy Semantic Maps for Robot Exploration
- **Room:** 333

[Project page](https://capability-aware-traversability.github.io)

### HiveBoard: An Open, Modular, 3D-Printed Benchmark of Industrial Mechanisms for Robotic and Prosthetic Manipulation

HiveBoard will be presented at two workshops:

- **[The 1st International Workshop on Industrial Applications of Robot Learning](https://aistairc.github.io/IROS2026-workshop/)** — Sunday, September 27. Workshop starts at 8:30 a.m., Room 407.
- **[Reproducible Benchmarking of Robotic Grasping and Manipulation: From Advanced AI to Generalized Humanoid Intelligence](https://sites.google.com/view/rgmcw2026)** — Thursday, October 1. Workshop runs from 1:30 to 5:30 p.m., Rooms 315 and 316.

[HiveBoard project page](https://hiveboard-bench.github.io)

All times are local to Pittsburgh.

Have a great IROS, everyone!

[HiveBoard](https://hiveboard-bench.github.io) received the **Best Poster Presentation Award** at the [1st International Workshop on Industrial Applications of Robot Learning](https://aistairc.github.io/IROS2026-workshop/) at IROS 2026 in Pittsburgh!

Thank you to all contributors across the participating labs, and to Floris Erich and the workshop organizers for the recognition. A special thanks to Gianluca Capezzuto for his brilliant presentation and for representing our team at IROS.

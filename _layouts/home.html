Ricardo V. Godoy

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of São Paulo (USP), Center for Robotics. My research focuses on manipulation, locomanipulation, shared autonomy, and human–robot interaction, with an emphasis on systems evaluated on physical robots.

I am currently technical lead for manipulation and locomanipulation in an industry-funded field robotics project. I also serve as an Associate Editor for IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters in the area of Robot Learning.

Research

My research focuses on robotic manipulation and locomanipulation, teleoperation and shared control, and human–robot interaction. I work with vision, EMG, lightmyography, gaze, gestures, and natural language to develop perception, planning, learning, and control methods for field, industrial, and assistive robots. I evaluate these systems on physical hardware, under real-time and safety constraints.

Ricardo V. Godoy

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of São Paulo (USP), Center for Robotics, where I lead the manipulation and locomanipulation team in an industry-funded project on robotic inspection and maintenance of offshore platforms.

I develop perception and control methods for manipulation and locomanipulation, including grasp estimation from partial RGB-D observations, collision-aware motion planning, and shared control for teleoperated robots.

I also develop EMG, lightmyography, and vision-based interfaces for teleoperation, shared control, and the control of robotic hands and prostheses.
